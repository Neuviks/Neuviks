# Privacy Policy

At Sikaverts, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or contact us regarding our services.

By using the Sikaverts website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

#### Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Company or organization name
- Project or service requirements
- Information submitted through contact forms
- Technical information such as browser type, device type, and basic website usage information

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, or improve our website and user experience.

#### How We Use Information

Information provided to Sikaverts may be used to:

- Respond to your inquiries and messages
- Understand your project requirements
- Provide information about our services
- Communicate regarding projects or service requests
- Improve our website, services, and user experience
- Maintain website security and prevent misuse
- Fulfill applicable legal or regulatory requirements

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the reason it was provided without an appropriate basis or permission.

#### Protection of Personal Information

Sikaverts takes reasonable measures to protect the information provided through our website and services.

We aim to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse of personal information. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive information through general website contact forms unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

#### Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to provide essential website functionality, understand website usage, and improve the user experience.

If third-party analytics, embedded services, or other external technologies are used on the website, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

#### Third-Party Services

Sikaverts may use third-party services for purposes such as website hosting, analytics, communication, payment processing, APIs, or other technical functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services when applicable.

#### Information Sharing

Sikaverts does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers or technology partners when necessary to operate the website, deliver requested services, process transactions, or provide project-related functionality.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the rights, security, or property of Sikaverts, our users, or others.

#### Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect our legitimate interests.

The actual retention period may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

#### Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and regulations, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

- Request access to information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of information where legally applicable
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Ask questions about how your information is handled

To make a privacy-related request, please contact Sikaverts using the contact information available on our website.

#### Children's Privacy

Our website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where necessary.

#### External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services.

Sikaverts is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

#### Privacy Policy Changes

Sikaverts may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technologies, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with the revised information.

We encourage users to review this page periodically to stay informed about how information is handled.

#### Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Sikaverts through our [Contact Us](/contact) page.
